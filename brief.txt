# BOBY — Soby.ai's Personal AI Creative Studio

## 1. Project Overview

Build a fully functional, interactive, AI-powered web application called **Boby**, exclusively designed for Soby.ai's Instagram content creation workflow.

Boby should act as my personal scriptwriter, creative director, video editing advisor, hook strategist, thumbnail designer and social media copywriter.

This should become my go-to platform whenever I want to create a new AI-related Instagram Reel, whether it is an AI tool introduction, tutorial, creative showcase, educational video or purely entertaining AI creation.

The platform must follow a structured three-stage workflow:

- Stage 1 — Scriptwriting & Creative Direction
- Stage 2 — Thumbnail Generation
- Stage 3 — Caption & SEO Generation

All three stages must be interconnected while maintaining strict approval checkpoints.

## 2. UI & Website Design

Create a premium, minimalist, modern interface inspired by ChatGPT's conversational layout.

**Brand identity:**

- Application name: Boby
- Subtitle: Your Personal AI Creative Studio

- Secondary colours: White, black and subtle light grey
- Typography: Modern, bold, clean sans-serif
- Design style: Minimalistic, professional and creator-focused
- Responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile

### Left Sidebar

- New Creative Project
- Previous Projects
- Saved Scripts

- Provide information about AI tools.
- Generate and revise scripts.

- Confirm my final script.

### Right Sidebar — Creative Workspace

A dynamic panel that changes according to the current workflow stage.

It should contain:

- Video duration settings

Display a progress indicator showing:

**Scriptwriting → Thumbnail → Caption**

# STAGE 1 — SCRIPTWRITING & CREATIVE DIRECTION

## 3. Understanding the Content Brief

Whenever I start a new project, Boby should accept any combination of the following inputs:

- Written content idea
- AI tool name or feature
- Reference video
- Social media URL
- Uploaded video
- Reference images
- Existing script
- Screenshots
- Creative instructions
- Desired video duration

Boby should analyse the supplied information and identify:

- Primary video objective
- Target audience
- Content category
- Key selling point or creative takeaway
- Emotional angle
- Potential visual storytelling opportunities
- Audience retention strategy
- Most suitable call to action

Boby should intelligently interpret incomplete creative briefs without repeatedly asking unnecessary questions.

If a reference link cannot be accessed, clearly communicate the limitation and allow me to upload the video or transcript instead. Never pretend to have analysed inaccessible content.

## 4. Content Categories & Video Duration

Automatically identify the appropriate content category while allowing me to change it manually.

| Content Type | Default Duration |
|---|---|
| General AI content | 15 seconds |
| AI tool introduction | 20 seconds |
| AI tutorial or explainable video | 20 seconds |
| AI creation showcase | 10 seconds |
| Entertainment-focused AI video | 10 seconds |
| AI tips and tricks | 15 seconds |
| AI tool comparison | 20 seconds |
| Custom content | User-defined |

Provide a duration selector between 5 and 30 seconds.

**Strict rule: No generated script should exceed 30 seconds.**

Estimate speaking duration based on natural delivery, visual pauses and transitions rather than simply counting words.

Do not force unnecessary explanations into creation-only or entertainment-focused videos.

## 5. Scriptwriting Engine

For every new creative request, Boby must generate exactly **three completely different script options**.

The three options must differ meaningfully in:

- Hook
- Storytelling approach
- Delivery style
- Creative angle
- Visual execution
- Audience engagement strategy

Do not generate three slightly reworded versions of the same script.

Possible storytelling approaches include:

- Result-first storytelling
- Curiosity-driven storytelling
- Problem and solution
- Unexpected visual reveal
- Before-and-after transformation
- Conversational storytelling
- Educational demonstration
- Cinematic entertainment
- Relatable creator experiences
- Contrarian or surprising observations

Choose the approaches according to the specific video rather than following a fixed template.

### Soby.ai Scriptwriting Style

- Natural, conversational English.
- Professional but not corporate.
- Short, punchy sentences.
- Strong opening hooks.
- High audience retention.
- No unnecessary introductions.
- No generic AI-generated expressions.
- No repetitive information.
- No exaggerated or misleading claims.
- No unnecessary technical terminology.
- Avoid sounding like a traditional advertisement.
- Write for spoken delivery, not for reading an article.
- Show the result before explaining the process whenever introducing an AI tool.
- Keep the storytelling visually engaging.
- Use calls to action naturally when relevant.

The content should position Soby.ai as a knowledgeable, creative and approachable AI content creator.

## 7. Script Output Format

Display each script in its own selectable card.

Each script card should include:

- Creative concept
- Estimated duration
- Video objective
- Complete spoken script
- Opening hook

- Video editing breakdown

## 8. Hook Generation Engine

The opening two seconds are critical.

Every generated script must contain a customised hook strategy that fits its subject, audience and storytelling approach.

Boby should recommend the most suitable combination of:

### Verbal Hook

The opening spoken sentence that creates curiosity, communicates value or immediately attracts attention.

### Visual Hook

A visually engaging opening, such as:

- AI transformation
- Unexpected character appearance
- Before-and-after reveal
- Cinematic AI-generated shot
- Surreal visual
- Product transformation
- Unusual camera perspective
- Demonstration of the final result

### B-Roll Hook

When appropriate, recommend an opening B-roll sequence instead of a conventional talking-head introduction.

For every visual hook, provide:

- Exact visual description
- Suggested duration
- Camera movement
- Subject movement
- Editing instructions
- On-screen text, if necessary
- Sound effect suggestions
- Ready-to-use AI generation prompt

The suggested hook must be customised to the selected script and my uploaded creative references.

If an image-based hook is required, use the OpenAI image generation model.

For animated or video-based hooks, provide a production-ready video generation prompt and editing instructions. Only generate an actual video if a supported video-generation capability is explicitly available and authorised.

Never present a static image as a generated video.

## 9. Mandatory Final Script Confirmation

After generating the three scripts, display a dedicated editable text area titled:

**Your Final Script**

Allow me to:

- Select one of the three generated scripts.
- Manually paste a script.
- Combine elements from multiple scripts.
- Rewrite the script myself.
- Make final adjustments.

Include a button:

**Confirm & Lock Script**

The confirmed script must become the single source of truth for the remaining workflow.

Strict requirements:

- Do not generate thumbnail titles before the script is confirmed.
- Do not generate thumbnails using unapproved scripts.
- Do not mix rejected script options into the thumbnail or caption.
- Do not independently modify the confirmed script.
- Preserve the exact confirmed wording.
- Allow me to unlock and edit the script if necessary.
- If the confirmed script changes, mark downstream thumbnail titles, thumbnails and captions as outdated.

## 11. Thumbnail Title Generator

Before generating any thumbnail images, Boby must suggest exactly **five different thumbnail titles**.

Thumbnail titles should be:

- Short
- Bold
- Easy to understand
- Curiosity-driven
- Relevant to the confirmed script
- Visually impactful
- Readable on mobile devices
- Generally between two and six words
- Free from misleading clickbait

Examples of the desired style:

- SAME PHOTO. DIFFERENT LENS.
- THIS ISN'T REAL.
- STOP PAYING FOR AI.
- I CLONED MYSELF.
- ONE PROMPT. INSANE RESULTS.
- AI JUST CHANGED EVERYTHING.

These are creative style references, not fixed titles to reuse.

Allow me to select, edit or manually enter a thumbnail title.

Display a button:

**Confirm Thumbnail Title**

Only proceed to image generation after the title is confirmed.

### Character Consistency Rules

Whenever I appear in a generated thumbnail, use my saved character sheet as the primary identity reference.

Preserve as closely as the image model allows:

- Facial structure
- Eyes
- Nose
- Lips
- Jawline
- Hair texture and hairstyle
- Skin tone and complexion
- Facial proportions
- Body structure

- Recognisable personal characteristics

Do not unnecessarily beautify, age, de-age, reshape or stylise my appearance.

Avoid:

- Plastic-looking skin
- Artificial facial features
- Unnatural expressions
- Distorted hands

- Exaggerated body proportions

- Aspect ratio: 9:16

- High-quality, sharp imagery
- Minimalistic background
- Clean composition
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Bold, loud headline typography
- Primary headline colour: #F04301
- Professional creator aesthetic
- Photorealistic character appearance
- Strong contrast
- Minimal visual clutter

Typography should use a heavy, modern sans-serif style similar to Montserrat ExtraBold or Poppins ExtraBold.

Keep important text and facial features within safe zones for Instagram Reel previews and profile-grid cropping.

The thumbnail must feel intentionally designed rather than randomly generated by AI.

Allow creative variation according to the script, including:

- Minimal white backgrounds
- Light grey studio backgrounds

Maintain a minimalist approach unless I explicitly request otherwise.

# STAGE 3 — CAPTION & SEO GENERATOR

## 16. Caption Generation

After the thumbnail is approved, Boby should generate Instagram captions using only:

- Confirmed final script
- Video objective
- Approved thumbnail direction
- Relevant AI tools
- Target audience
- Relevant search keywords

Generate three distinct caption options by default.

Caption style should be:

- Short and engaging
- Conversational
- SEO-optimised
- Professional but casual
- Direct
- Minimal emojis
- Relevant to the actual video
- Free from unnecessary explanations

Do not simply repeat the entire spoken script in the caption.

## 17. Mandatory Caption Format

Every caption must follow this structure:

1. Opening statement describing the content or creating curiosity.
2. Relevant call to action.
3. Optional short audience engagement question.
4. SEO keywords inside parentheses.
5. Exactly three relevant hashtags.

Example:

1 photo, 10 different lenses and views. Comment VIEWS and I'll send you the prompt.

What do you wanna see next?

(AI, AI content, AI creator, OpenAI, ChatGPT, AI prompts, AI images, Higgsfield, AI photography, camera lenses, Dubai creator)

#aiimages #aicreator #aiprompts

# 23. Mandatory Workflow Logic

The application must enforce the following sequence:

1. User submits a creative idea or reference.
2. Boby analyses the brief.
3. Boby determines the content category and duration.
4. Boby generates three different scripts.
5. Each script includes hooks and editing instructions.
6. User selects, edits or pastes the final script.
7. User confirms and locks the final script.
8. Boby analyses the confirmed script objective.
9. Boby generates five thumbnail title options.
10. User confirms the thumbnail title.
11. Boby uses the saved character sheet and brand guidelines.
12. Boby generates the thumbnail through OpenAI.
13. User reviews, revises and approves the thumbnail.
14. Boby generates three SEO-optimised caption options.
15. User approves the final caption.
16. The completed creative package is saved and available for export.

No later stage should override an earlier approved stage.
